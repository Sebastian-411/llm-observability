# Inside a YC batch

A YC batch lasts approximately **three months** and ends with Demo Day. Here is
what happens during that time.

## Office Hours
Each company is assigned a **group partner** who runs **weekly office hours**.
These are short (~20 minute) one-on-one meetings to discuss the week's
biggest problem: customer acquisition, hiring, product, fundraising, etc.

There are also **larger group office hours** with 4–6 companies and a partner,
where founders share progress and trade advice.

## Tuesday Dinners
A YC tradition started in 2005: every Tuesday, founders gathered for dinner to
hear from a guest speaker — typically a successful founder, investor or
operator. Speakers have included Mark Zuckerberg, Jeff Bezos, Reid Hoffman,
Peter Thiel, Brian Chesky, and Patrick Collison. Talks are off the record.

## Bookface
**Bookface** is YC's internal social network, accessible only to YC alumni.
It serves as a directory of founders, a forum for asking questions, and a
warm-intro engine. With more than 10,000 active members, Bookface is widely
considered one of YC's most valuable long-term assets.

## Standard advice
YC partners famously give a small set of repeated pieces of advice, including:
- **"Make something people want."** — the YC motto, printed on every T-shirt.
- **"Talk to your users."**
- **"Do things that don't scale."** (PG's essay of the same name.)
- **"Launch now."** — most founders launch too late.
- **"Default alive vs. default dead."** — know your runway and burn rate
  at all times.

## Demo Day
At the end of the batch, each company pitches to ~1,000 investors over one or
two days. As of 2023, Demo Day is **online-first** for investors with a
parallel in-person event. Pitches are limited to **~2 minutes** with a single
slide. The goal is not to close investors on stage, but to get meetings.

## Post-batch
After Demo Day, companies typically raise a **seed round** in the following
weeks. YC partners help with intros, deal-term advice, and follow-on
investments via the YC Continuity Fund for later stages (the Continuity Fund
was wound down in 2023; YC now focuses exclusively on early stage).
