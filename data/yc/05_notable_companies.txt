Notable Y Combinator alumni companies

Airbnb (Winter 2009)
  Founders: Brian Chesky, Joe Gebbia, Nathan Blecharczyk.
  Marketplace for short-term lodging. IPO'd December 2020 at ~$47B.
  Famously rejected by many investors before YC; PG's wife Jessica Livingston
  championed them.

Stripe (Summer 2009)
  Founders: Patrick and John Collison.
  Payments infrastructure for the internet. Most valuable private YC company,
  last valued around $65B (2024 tender). Originally called "/dev/payments".

Dropbox (Summer 2007)
  Founder: Drew Houston (later joined by Arash Ferdowsi).
  File sync and storage. Public on NASDAQ since 2018 (ticker DBX).

Reddit (Summer 2005 — YC's first batch)
  Founders: Steve Huffman, Alexis Ohanian.
  Social news aggregator. Acquired by Condé Nast in 2006; spun back out;
  IPO'd in March 2024 at ~$6.4B.

Coinbase (Summer 2012)
  Founders: Brian Armstrong, Fred Ehrsam.
  Cryptocurrency exchange. Direct-listed on NASDAQ in April 2021.

DoorDash (Summer 2013)
  Founders: Tony Xu, Stanley Tang, Andy Fang, Evan Moore.
  Food delivery. IPO'd December 2020 at ~$72B.

Instacart (Summer 2012)
  Founder: Apoorva Mehta.
  Grocery delivery. IPO'd September 2023 (Maplebear Inc, ticker CART).

Twitch (Winter 2007, originally Justin.tv)
  Founders: Justin Kan, Emmett Shear, Michael Seibel, Kyle Vogt.
  Live streaming. Acquired by Amazon in 2014 for $970M.

GitLab (Winter 2015)
  Founders: Sid Sijbrandij, Dmitriy Zaporozhets.
  DevOps platform. IPO'd October 2021.

Rappi (Winter 2016)
  Founders: Simón Borrero, Sebastián Mejía, Felipe Villamarín.
  Latin American on-demand delivery. Largest YC company in Latin America.

PagerDuty (Summer 2010)
  Founders: Alex Solomon, Andrew Miklas, Baskar Puvanathasan.
  Incident response platform. IPO'd April 2019.

Cruise (Winter 2014)
  Founder: Kyle Vogt (also Twitch cofounder).
  Self-driving cars. Acquired by GM in 2016 for ~$1B.

OpenAI was NOT a YC company. Sam Altman led YC during the period he also
cofounded OpenAI (2015), but OpenAI was founded as a non-profit research lab
and never went through the YC batch program.
