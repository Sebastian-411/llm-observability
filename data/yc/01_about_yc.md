# What is Y Combinator?

Y Combinator (YC) is an American startup accelerator and seed-stage venture firm
founded in **March 2005** by Paul Graham, Jessica Livingston, Robert Tapan
Morris, and Trevor Blackwell. It is headquartered in **Mountain View,
California**, and is widely credited with creating the modern accelerator model.

## Mission
YC's stated mission is to **help founders build great companies**. It runs two
batches per year — Winter and Summer — funding and mentoring early-stage
startups in exchange for a small equity stake.

## How it works
- Founders apply online; the application form is famously short.
- Accepted founders relocate (or for recent batches, attend in-person in the
  Bay Area) for a **~3-month batch program**.
- During the batch they meet weekly with YC partners ("office hours"), attend
  dinners with founders and prominent guests, and prepare for Demo Day.
- The program culminates in **Demo Day**: each startup pitches to a curated
  audience of investors.

## Standard deal (as of 2022)
YC invests **$500,000 per company** under a two-part instrument:
- **$125,000** for **7%** of the company (post-money SAFE).
- **$375,000** on an **uncapped SAFE** with the "Most-Favored-Nation" provision
  (the MFN SAFE), which converts at the next priced round's valuation.

Before 2022 the standard deal was $125k for 7%; the additional $375k MFN was
added to give companies more runway.

## Scale
By 2024, YC had funded **over 5,000 companies** with a combined valuation
exceeding **$600 billion**. Notable YC alumni include Airbnb, Dropbox, Stripe,
Reddit, Coinbase, Instacart, DoorDash, Twitch, Cruise, Scribd, GitLab,
Rappi, PagerDuty, and Zapier.

## Leadership timeline
- **2005–2014**: Paul Graham led YC as president.
- **2014–2019**: Sam Altman became president, expanded YC's footprint
  (YC Research, YC China, YC Continuity).
- **2019–2023**: Geoff Ralston was president.
- **2023–present**: **Garry Tan** is president and CEO. He has refocused YC
  on early-stage software startups, AI, and in-person batches.
